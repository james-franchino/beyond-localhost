## About

Beyond Localhost is a website dedicated to my personal homelab,
self-hosted applications, and my interest in Linux, networking,
and technology.

## Why I Chose This Topic

Self-hosting is one of my favorite hobbies. I enjoy experimenting
with new software, managing my own servers, and learning how
different technologies work together. I chose this topic because
it gives me an opportunity to share something I enjoy
while documenting what I've built and learned along the way.
